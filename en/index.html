About

Thomas Simão

I started out running Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns at EasyLounge, then at Groupe Foncier de France. I quickly realized that content was the most underrated lever in Ads performance.


Today I'm Traffic Manager at Son-Vidéo.com, where I manage a €260K annual budget across Google Ads and Meta Ads, and produce the video creatives in-house, entirely with AI. Synthetic UGC, product motion design, photo animation: everything is made with Higgsfield and the Seedance model, delivered in 48h, no studio and no shoot.


What drives me: proving that well-thought-out AI content can perform as well as (or better than) a traditional production. My best result: a €1.54 CPA and a 4.05% CTR on 100% AI UGC.
