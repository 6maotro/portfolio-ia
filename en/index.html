Technical craft · Client project

Tri For Fun, a store filmed without a camera

Triathlon & running store · Barcelona

From 3D mockup to the real store, no shoot

Top: a 3D mockup of the store. Bottom: the same shot turned into photorealistic video with AI.

A controlled render, not AI left to chance. An AI inventing a scene on its own changes the shelves, moves the walls and warps the products from one shot to the next. Here, the 3D mockup is the skeleton: layout, volumes and camera movement are locked in advance, and the AI only handles materials and light.

Result: the client recognizes their store, every shot stays consistent with the next, and any take can be redone without starting from scratch.

3D mockup · Higgsfield Genjutsu
01

The approach

Instead of a standard cold message, I reached out to the store with a video already made for them, built from their own retail space.

02

The production

First the 3D mockup of the store, where I frame every shot as on a real set: camera path, pacing, point of view. Then the AI turns these shots into realistic images while respecting the mockup's geometry. The store is showcased without closing its doors or organizing a shoot.

03

The result

The demo won the project. I now handle the store's video communication.

Storytelling · Concept ad

Delta Fraternité, a story in 30 seconds

Concept ad · Independent clothing brand

Delta Fraternité · “That's me, yesterday”

A 30-second concept ad for Delta Fraternité, an independent clothing brand. It features the DREAMCHASER and NO SLEEP.

A story before a product. The brand talks about entrepreneurship and pushing yourself. Rather than showing clothes on a plain background, the ad tells a face-off: the hero confronts yesterday's version of himself, with one idea, being better than the day before.

The clothes aren't set dressing, they carry the message. The viewer remembers an emotion, and the emotion brings them back to the brand.

Concept ad · 100% AI
01

Start from the brand's DNA

I first studied what the brand and its founder talk about on social media: hard work, discipline, the drive to push further. The concept comes from there, not from a template.

02

Build a story in 30 seconds

A starting point, a tension, a resolution. Every shot moves the story forward, and the tagline lands when the viewer has already felt it.

03

Make the product the hero

Both pieces stay readable and accurate from shot to shot. The story serves the garment instead of hiding it.

About

Thomas Simão

I started out running Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns at EasyLounge, then at Groupe Foncier de France. I quickly realized that content was the most underrated lever in Ads performance.


At Son-Vidéo.com, as Traffic Manager, I managed a €260K annual budget across Google Ads and Meta Ads, and produced the video creatives in-house, entirely with AI. Synthetic UGC, product motion design, photo animation: everything made with Higgsfield and the Seedance model, delivered in 48h, no studio and no shoot.


What drives me: proving that well-thought-out AI content can perform as well as (or better than) a traditional production. My best result: a €1.54 CPA and a 4.05% CTR on 100% AI UGC.


Today, I bring this method to e-commerce brands and agencies, from Barcelona. I work in French, English, Spanish and Portuguese.
